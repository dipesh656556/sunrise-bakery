Privacy Policy

Sunrise Bakery Manager (the "App") · Last updated: 20 November 2026

The App is a private business tool used by the owner and staff of Sunrise Bakery, Nepal, to manage cake orders, customers, income and expenses. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we collect and store

  • Staff login: the email address and password of staff accounts, used only to sign in (managed by Google Firebase Authentication).
  • Business records entered by staff: customer names, phone numbers, birthdays, order details (cake, date, price, advance), and income/expense entries.

The App does not collect location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, advertising IDs or any data in the background beyond what staff enter.

How data is used

Data is used only to run the bakery: to show orders and reports, to prepare WhatsApp messages (cake ready, birthday wishes, yearly reminders) that staff choose to send, and to sync data between the bakery's devices. Messages are sent by the staff member through WhatsApp; the App does not send messages automatically on its own.

Where data is stored

Data is stored on the device and in the bakery's own Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) project so that authorised staff devices stay in sync. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Only signed-in staff can read or write it.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third parties or advertisers. Data is processed only by Google Firebase as our cloud service provider. The App contains no ads and no analytics trackers.

Retention and deletion

Records are kept while the bakery needs them. A customer or staff member can ask us to delete their data at any time by contacting us below; we will delete it from the App and from the cloud database. The owner can also delete any record inside the App.

Children

The App is intended for business use by adults and is not directed to children.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above will change when we do.
